Frequently asked

The widget doesn't show the new drawing. Widgets refresh on their own after a moment; long-press the widget to pick its style.

Family members don't see the drawings. Check that everyone is signed in to iCloud and accepted the invitation sent from the Family area.

Restore a subscription. Open the Family area, then Draw Your Family Plus, and tap Restore.
