Help & FAQ

How do I unlock Lena's phone?

You don't need to guess. Even a locked phone shows a stranger what to do in an emergency, and that's exactly the kind of information that can tell you a lot about its owner. Look carefully at what the lock screen lets you open. The code is something personal.

Her friends don't answer, or only answer briefly.

Maya, Jess and Tom reply through an AI that runs entirely on your iPhone. It needs a one-time download of about 0.9 GB, which waits for Wi-Fi by default. Check its progress in the game's Settings app, under “Friends' AI”. It keeps going in the background. Without it, her friends still answer, just more briefly.

The unknown number isn't writing back.

That's part of the game: he only writes on the quarter hour (:00, :15, :30, :45), in real time, and stays online for two minutes. Anything he has to say waits for his next slot.

I'm not getting notifications.

The story keeps going when the game is closed: his messages arrive as notifications. If you declined them, open the game's Settings app and tap “Allow notifications”, or turn them on in your iPhone's Settings → Notifications → Lena's Phone. Notifications are created on your phone; nothing is sent from a server.

I failed a riddle. What now?

A wrong answer or a timer running out ends the game, but you can try that riddle again right away. If you're stuck, wait a little: her friends will nudge you, one clue at a time.

How do I start the story over?

Open the Settings app inside the game and tap “Start over”. This erases your clues and conversations on this device.

How do I change the language?

The game follows your iPhone's language by default (English or French). You can also pick one in the game's Settings app.
