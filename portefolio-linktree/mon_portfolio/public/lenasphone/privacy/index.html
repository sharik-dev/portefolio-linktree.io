Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

In short: Lena's Phone collects no data. No account, no analytics, no ads, no tracking. Everything you do in the game stays on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. Lena's Phone does not collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal data. There is no sign-up and no account. The game contains no analytics, no crash-reporting service, no advertising and no third-party tracking tools.

What stays on your device

Your progress, the clues you find and what you write to the characters in the game are saved only on your device. They never leave it. Deleting the app, or choosing “Start over” in the game's Settings app, erases them.

The friends' AI (optional)

Lena's friends answer you through an AI model that runs entirely on your device. If you agree to it, the game downloads this model once (about 0.9 GB) from Hugging Face, a public model-hosting service. This download is the only network request the game makes. It contains no personal data: no account, no identifier, nothing you have typed. As with any download, the hosting service technically receives your IP address to deliver the file; its own privacy policy applies to that.

Once downloaded, the model works offline. Your messages to the characters are processed on your device and are never sent anywhere. You can delete the model at any time in the game's Settings app.

Notifications

The game uses local notifications only: they are scheduled on your device so the story can reach you when the app is closed. No push server is involved and no device token is collected. You can turn them off at any time in your device's Settings.

Children

Lena's Phone is a thriller intended for players aged 16 and over. Since the game collects no data from anyone, it does not knowingly collect data from children either.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published on this page with an updated date.
