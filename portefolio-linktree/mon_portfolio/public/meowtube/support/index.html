Support

Need help with MeowTube? Write to us at sharikmohamed8@gmail.com, or use Home › Feedback in the app. We usually reply within a few days.

How do I hide Shorts and Reels?

There's nothing to set up: MeowTube removes Shorts, Reels and short-video feeds automatically on the sites you open from its Home screen.

How do I set a daily time limit?

Open Settings in the app, turn on the session gauge and choose your daily limit. With MeowTube Pro, the app can also lock when the limit is reached.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are handled by Apple: open the iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions › MeowTube. You can cancel at any time.

I changed phone: how do I get Pro back?

Open Settings in the app, then MeowTube Pro › Restore purchases, with the same Apple account.

How do I change the language?

Open Settings › Language. MeowTube is available in English, French, Spanish, Arabic and Hindi.

How do I delete my account?

Signing in is optional. To delete your account, open Settings › Account › Delete account.
